Day 1: Ancient Legends and Majestic Vistas (Airport/Negombo - Chilaw - Sigiriya - Dambulla)

Highlights: Manawari Temple (Ramayana Trail Site), Sigiriya Rock Fortress (UNESCO World Heritage Site)

Morning: Pick-up from Airport or Negombo. Complimentary breakfast, then head to Manawari Temple in Chilaw.

A Note on the Manawari Temple Visit:

A puja is a wonderful opportunity to immerse yourself in local culture and religious practices. Please let us know in advance if you plan to attend the puja.

Afternoon: Visit the iconic Sigiriya Rock Fortress, a marvel of ancient engineering and art with breathtaking views.

Preparing for Your Sigiriya Rock Fortress Visit:

  • Wear a hat with a chin strap or cap.
  • Wear comfortable shoes with good grip.
  • Bring a light jacket or windbreaker.
  • Carry a water bottle.
  • Keep belongings secure near edges.

Evening: Check-in to your hotel in Dambulla and enjoy a delicious dinner.

Meals Included:

  • Breakfast at hotel.
  • Dinner at hotel (buffet or à la carte).
  • Vegetarian options available.

📅 Schedule for Day 1:

  • 7:00 AM – 7:45 AM: Pick-up with breakfast
  • 8:00 AM – 9:30 AM: Travel to Chilaw
  • 9:30 AM – 11:30 AM: Visit Manawari Temple & puja
  • 11:30 AM – 1:00 PM: Travel to Dambulla
  • 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM: Lunch in Dambulla
  • 2:00 PM – 6:00 PM: Explore Sigiriya Rock Fortress
  • 6:00 PM: Hotel check-in
  • 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM: Dinner at hotel

Day 2: Dambulla to Trincomalee

Highlights: Thirukoneswaram Kovil, Kanniya Hot Springs

Morning: Scenic journey from Dambulla to Trincomalee. Visit Thirukoneswaram Kovil, dedicated to Lord Shiva, dramatically situated on a cliff.

A Note on Temple Etiquette:

Please dress respectfully, covering shoulders and knees. Footwear must be removed before entering the temple grounds.

Afternoon: Visit Kanniya Hot Springs. Famous wells believed to be created by Ravana’s trident strike, offering relaxation and reflection.

Preparing for Your Kanniya Hot Springs Visit:

  • Bring a small hand towel.
  • Wear comfortable, easy-to-remove footwear.
  • Stay hydrated.

Evening: Return to your hotel in Dambulla. Enjoy dinner and relax.

Meals Included:

  • Breakfast at hotel.
  • Dinner at hotel (buffet or à la carte).
  • Vegetarian options available.

📅 Schedule for Day 2:

  • 7:00 AM – 8:00 AM: Breakfast at hotel
  • 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM: Travel to Thirukoneswaram Kovil
  • 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM: Explore temple & puja
  • 1:00 PM – 2:00 PM: Lunch break
  • 2:00 PM – 2:30 PM: Visit Kanniya Hot Springs
  • 2:30 PM – 5:00 PM: Travel back to Dambulla
  • 5:00 PM: Hotel check-in
  • 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM: Dinner at hotel

Day 4: Spiritual Trials and Mythical Caves (NuwaraEliya - Ella - NuwaraEliya)

Highlights: Divurumpola Temple (Ramayana Trail Site), Ravana Cave (Ramayana Trail Site)

Morning: Your day begins with a significant pilgrimage to the Divurumpola Temple in Welimada.This site is of immense importance on the Ramayana trail as it is believed to be the location of the 'Agni Pariksha', or the Trial by Fire, which Sita underwent to prove her chastity after her rescue. Even today, the temple is a place where sacred oaths are taken, as it is believed that any false vow made here will be exposed by the power of truth, a legacy of Sita's trial. Visiting this temple offers a deep sense of connection to the epic's most pivotal moments.

Afternoon: We will then travel to Ella, a scenic town famous for its natural beauty and legendary connections. Here, we'll embark on a short trek to the famous.Ravana CaveThis cave is part of a fascinating network of natural and man-made tunnels that once served as a secret passage for King Ravana. According to local lore, this is where Ravana hid Sita, adding another layer of intrigue to the story. The hike itself is rewarding, leading to a stunning opening that reveals a breathtaking view of the surrounding landscape, providing a true sense of adventure.

Evening: After a fulfilling day of historical and spiritual exploration, we will return to Nuwara Eliya for a relaxing evening at your hotel. Dinner will be served, allowing you to reflect on the legends and beauty you have experienced

Meals Included:

  • Complimentary breakfast is included daily.
  • Dinner is included at your hotel, offering either an à la carte or buffet option.
  • Most of the hotels and restaurants on this tour will be offering vegetarian meals throughout your stay.

  • 10:30 PM – 11:30 PM:Travel to ella
  • 11:30 PM – 1:00 PM : Trek and explore Ravana Cave
